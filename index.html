[Donate](#donate)[Explore](#about) **Food**Fighting hunger **Children**Protecting the vulnerable **Families**Restoring hope **Community**Sustainable development Children from Baringo families gathered on dry ground ## About Us Seeds of Hope Baringo is a community-driven initiative dedicated to supporting drought-affected families, vulnerable children and struggling households in Baringo County. We work through partnerships, donations and collective action, because we believe every act of kindness can change a life. ### Our Mission To fight hunger, restore hope, empower families and promote sustainable community development through partnerships, donations and collective action. ### Our Vision A Baringo where no child goes to bed hungry and every family has the support to recover from drought and thrive. ## Our Programs Practical help that reaches families where they are. ### Food Relief Food packs and meals for households hit hardest by drought. ### Child Support Nutrition, school essentials and care for vulnerable children. ### Family Empowerment Helping struggling households rebuild their livelihoods. ### Community Development Partnering with locals on water, farming and long-term resilience. ## Life in Baringo South Long dry spells have left the ground bare and families struggling to put food on the table.
